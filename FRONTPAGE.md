Redstone⁴'s Vehicles is a datapack that adds three new vehicles to the game:

* Submarine
* Biplane
* Helicopter


# Items

**Blazing Fuel**: Universal fuel required by all vehicles.  Right click to add fuel to a vehicle.


# Vehicles

**Submarine**:
* WASD + mouse to move around
* Does not sink in water

**Biplane**:
* Left-click to toggle engine
* Forward/Backward to increase/decrease thrust

**Helicopter**:
* Left-click to toggle engine
* WASD + mouse to move around
* Jump to move upwards
